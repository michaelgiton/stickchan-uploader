Privacy Policy for Stick Chan Uploader

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

Stick Chan Uploader is a personal tool that uploads videos to the Stick Chan YouTube channel, at the channel owner's request, using the YouTube Data API.

What the app accesses

When you run an upload, the app accesses your YouTube channel to upload the video and set its title, description, tags, language and visibility. It does not read your emails, files, contacts or any other Google service.

How your data is stored

The login token that lets the app upload is stored securely and is used only to perform uploads you ask for. You can take away access at any time from your Google account settings.

What is shared

Nothing is sold or shared. Video files and details go only to YouTube, as needed to publish the video.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Stick Chan YouTube channel.
